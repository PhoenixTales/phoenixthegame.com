## How to play and how not to

**It is not wise to collect crap.**  
Looting everything is no longer possible and *useless* and would be irrational to try. The inventory limitation has been restored on purpose and the balancing accordingly.  
Even if you would be able to take everything and forcibly re-implement an unlimited inventory, it would still be useless, because each trader has (1) a very limited amount of ore himself and (2) his own restricted interests.  
Traders have no need for your crap and they don't want to buy from you so much that you get rich and they get poor. THEY are the traders, not you. They primarily want to sell you stuff and not buy from you, unless you have something really special that they need and if so, they tell you so.

**It doesn't serve you anything to kill everything on sight.**  
Killing everything is not necessarily to your advantage. Gothic was meant to be fully story focused. Therefore the gameplay can not in any way conflict the narrative. If there is a narrative urgency to continue with the story, the player cannot be rewarded, but rather must be punished, for wasting time on other things like randomly knocking down NPCs or killing Monsters for EXP. This is a stupid system and always was. Therefore...  
  * Knocking down or killing NPCs does not give you EXP. Not even killing Monsters gives you EXP (unless you kill the species for the first time → first experience). If you want to farm exp, go play some MMORPG.
  * In order to harmonise the gameplay with the narrative, experience is almost only given for missions and making progress in the story. Extra EXP is given for choosing gameplay solutions, answers etc. in accordance with ones class/guild. 

**Let the Game look as it's meant to be.**   
The very purpose of the Demo is providing you with an experience of what Gothic was meant to be and how it was meant to look. Installing another texture patch defeats this purpose directly. This Demo can also not be played with the DX11 Renderer, because it sucks. Play it as it is intended to look or don't play it.
* If anyone streams gameplay of the Demo or uploads videos of it with texture patches or renderers applied, he lands on a list and is likely to be catched by Shadows at night...
* He also will receive a big style malus for playing with bad taste which will likely result in the Sleeper laughing about him in the end.
