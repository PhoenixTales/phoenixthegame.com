Gothic v0.8 = Phoenix Demo 1

With each Demo of PHOENIX we go further back in time, realising more of the very different original vision of GOTHIC, that had "Phoenix" as its working title, only a fracture of which was realised by Piranha Bytes.

Demo 1 is all about Gothic v0.8, presenting a world and story as imagined in early to mid 2000, giving you a first glimpse into the radical vision of a game that never was.

[TEASER Video]

Attention: In this Demo there will be no newly written story content yet, it is focused on what PB had actually written themselves. That also means that many cut, named characters, haven't much or anything to say yet, since no dialogues have been written for them. We print info texts as placeholders to inform the player that something was supposed to be here that hasn't yet been realised. It is in Phoenix Demo 2 that we'll start to patch all these holes. A detailed account of each change can be found in our changelog.

Being in contact with several of the original devs (who have long lost the Gothic IP and also helped us to realise the extensive Gothic Archive), Demo 1 should be considered as a restored and heavily improved developer build based on their old material, giving insight into the latest stage of Piranha Bytes, but carefully reconstructed and fixed by us, before they changed the story and reduced the scope to save the release, since otherwise, in fact, GOTHIC would have been cancelled.

Features:

  • Game World: Overworld and underworld locations and layout corresponding to v0.82, including all the old locations at the surface and the original underground levels of the time: Abandoned Mine, Old Mine, Free Mine and Orc City.
  • Style: Overall visual aesthetics of the "late Alpha". Level textures, armour designs, interface and monster design.
  • Cut Content: Cut NPCs and dialogues from scripts and documents of the 0.8 era. Cut Missions, unused/unfinished Items and forgotten, broken or unfinished functions restored/fixed.
  • Story: Demo 1 includes most of the release version story, but follows the original chapter structure: Orientation Phase (CH1) → PrepareRitual (CH2) → OMFull (CH3) → FMTaken (CH4) → OrcAssault (CH5) → Nemesis (CH6). The old Story is included in a rudimentary form, with the before mentioned necessary content gaps and story skips that are intended in this Demo to show PB's latest stage. Not included yet are the varying guild paths and the four classes gameplay as intended earlier, which will be part of later versions of Phoenix.
  • Balancing: Completely different character and item stats, following the concept of that time, which remained almost unchanged from 1997 to mid 2000. Different Exp system to slowly pave the way towards the immersive sim roleplay via the four character classes to be realised in upcoming versions of Phoenix.
  • Music: Complete Alpha Soundtrack corresponding to v0.82.
  • Additional Cut Content: Inclusion of unused animations (as far as useable at this stage), sound effects and particle effects.
  • Ambient Events: The Alpha Screenshots that correspond to v0.8 to 0.9 very often presented scenarios to create more atmosphere and the illusion that the world is as alive as they promoted and imagined it to be (but never was). These situations were staged for promotional purposes and not actually in the game. Now many of them are.
  • Ingame "Cutscenes": Cutscenes from the Alpha would be at place in this Demo, but as of date we do not have access to the plethora of original cutscenes yet that were made for Gothic (only a little fracture of scenes from Demo v0.94k are known). Most players are not even aware of the existence of all these other cutscenes, as they have never been shown in public so far. They all had been removed in the last few months, when the game world and story had to be cut and rewritten.
  • Sequel Improvements: The Sequel introduced many improvements such as advanced Crafting, but implementing them requires diverse engine tweaks. We consider them nice to have, they may or may not be included in Demo 1.
  • Fixes: To provide a sound basis for Phoenix, a vast amount of bugs and other problems have been fixed in the scripts and animations of the base game, logical inconsistencies have been corrected, items and objects have been fixed, several douzens of objects remapped and sometimes remodeled.

Requirements: To play this Demo you need a copy of GOTHIC (2001), which Phoenix is based on. You can legally acquire it here. After the installation of GOTHIC you can install the PHOENIX Demo in your Gothic directory.
