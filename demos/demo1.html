Phoenix Demo 1

With each Demo of PHOENIX we go further back in time, realising more of the very different original vision of GOTHIC, that had "Phoenix" as its working title, of which only a fracture was presented in the release version.

Demo 1 is all about Gothic v0.8, presenting a world and story of Gothic as imagined in early to mid 2000, giving you a first glimpse into the radical vision of a game that never was.

Features:

  • Overworld: Old surface layout corresponding to v0.82, with the old path into the valley, the original Psi Temple, the Old Pass, the Old Temple (later "Monastery Ruins"), with a few additions necessary for story purposes, such as an early version of the Mountain Fortress with the later Sequel expansions.
  • Underworld: At this stage of Gothics development, the idea of an interconnected underworld through the natural caves had already been discarded, the additional levels of the Sleeper temple had also been discarded already at this time (all these things are reserved for later versions of Phoenix). This Demo includes the Abandoned Mine, the models of the Free Mine and the Old Mine are switched, as originally intended, the original Underground Orc City and the slightly different Orc Temple of that period.
  • Style: Overall visual aesthetics of the "late Alpha", e.g. in form of the textures of this time (such as level textures, armour designs, interface and the monster designs of that era).
  • NPCs: All cut NPCs from the time until ~v0.8 as far as known.
  • Story: In this Demo there will be no or almost no newly written story content/dialogues by us yet, because for this version we will focus very much on what PB had actually written themselves. That also means that many cut, named characters, haven't much to say, many do not have a role yet, since no dialogues were written for them; but all the cut dialogues from scripts or documents of that period are included and a few missions that were more or less complete but haven't been included in the end have been restored. / The story still includes most of the the release version story, but the overall structure of the story follow the plans of that time (Orientation Phase (CH1) ? PrepareRitual (CH2) ? OMFull (CH3) ? FMTaken (CH4) ? OrcAssault (CH5) ? Nemesis (CH6)). Everything from the Old Story is included here, but in a very rudimentary form and without all the separated guild paths and class gameplay as intended earlier, which will be part of later versions of Phoenix.
  • Balancing: The character and item values are all totally different, following the concept of that time; even v0.9 was still using the old attribute values, where characters did not have more than ~30 HP, ~15 Strength, ~30 Mana etc.
  • Music: Complete Alpha Soundtrack corresponding to v0.82.
  • Additional Cut Content: Inclusion of unused animations (as far as useable at this stage), sound effects and particle effects.
  • Ambient Events: So called ingame "Cutscenes" from the Alpha and advanced Crafting and some other improvements introduced with the Sequel would also be at place in this demo, but are considered nice to have and may be patched in over time. The Cutscenes are problematic since we do not have access to the plethora of original cutscenes that were made for Gothic and that most players are not even aware of, as they have never been shown. The Sequel improvements are problematic because they require engine tweaks as well. These things are on the list for this version, but can't be confirmed.

To play this Demo you need a copy of the base game GOTHIC (2001), which Phoenix is based on. You can legally acquire it here. After the installation of GOTHIC you can install the PHOENIX DEMO in the Gothic directory.
